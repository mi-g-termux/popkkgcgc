Drop your two music files into this folder using these EXACT names,
and they'll play automatically — no code editing needed:

  main-song.mp3    -> plays through the whole site, starting the
                       moment the gift box is tapped open
  letter-song.mp3  -> fades in the moment the envelope is opened
                       to read the letter (replacing main-song)

Notes:
- .mp3 works everywhere. If your file is a different format (.wav,
  .m4a), either convert it to .mp3, or open gift.html, search for
  CONFIG, and change the matching filename's ending to match your
  file (e.g. "audio/main-song.wav").
- Leaving either file out just means that track stays silent —
  nothing will break.
- A small 🔊 button in the top-right corner of the site lets her
  mute or unmute at any time.
